# Privacy and Research Boundaries

## Included

- original source code for a synthetic demonstration;
- fictional node and edge records;
- public architectural documentation;
- general research safeguards;
- unit tests and a CI workflow.

## Deliberately excluded

- Elliptic++ raw or derived records;
- real wallet or transaction identifiers;
- downloaded datasets and provider files;
- dissertation source and literature notes;
- unpublished benchmark tables and metrics;
- test predictions and evidence examples from real data;
- model checkpoints and vector indexes;
- tuned parameters and complete ablation designs;
- API keys, environment files, and local absolute paths.

## Disclosure policy

This repository demonstrates implementation competence while preserving the
novelty and integrity of ongoing dissertation research. Exact empirical claims
should be taken only from a future reviewed thesis or publication.

## Data provenance

The synthetic files in `examples/` were created solely for this repository.
They do not reproduce, sample, or transform any real financial dataset.

## Responsible interpretation

Labels such as `high_risk` and `low_risk` are fictional states in this demo.
In a real system, forensic labels may be incomplete, delayed, disputed, or
incorrect. Human review and legal safeguards remain mandatory.
